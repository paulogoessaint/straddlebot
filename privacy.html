Privacy policy

straddlebot backup is a private tool used by a single person, the owner of the Google account that authorises it.

  • What it accesses: only the owner's own Google Drive, to upload and update backup copies of the owner's files.
  • What it collects: nothing. The tool has no server; it runs on the owner's computer.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone.
  • Storage: files stay in the owner's Google Drive; the access token stays on the owner's computer.
  • Revoking access: the owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: use the developer contact listed on the app's Google consent screen.
